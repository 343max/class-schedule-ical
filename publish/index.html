How subscription works

A calendar subscription points at the URL above. Your calendar app periodically re-downloads it and updates your events automatically — add a schedule, fix a room, or change a holiday, and the change shows up without re-importing anything.

  • Apple Calendar: File → New Calendar Subscription → paste the URL (or just click Subscribe above).
  • Google Calendar: Settings → Add calendar → From URL → paste the https://… URL.

Note: calendar apps refresh subscriptions on their own schedule (Google can lag by up to a day), so changes are not instant.
